Will my name stay saved?

Yes. The site uses browser local storage so the name can be restored when you return on the same browser/device. Clearing site data removes it.

What are the FF ID products?

Prime 7 ID Max and Prime 8 ID Max. For the ID flow, payment route comes first, followed by the available level and rate.

What are the BGMI options?

LVL 70 ID and LVL 80 ID. Choose Full Payment or Half Payment first, then choose the level and one of the three listed rates.

What are the top-up options?

2 Year Membership and 5000 Diamonds. Top-ups use the Full Payment route only; no ID-check or half-payment section appears.
